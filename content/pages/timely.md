title: "TIMELY tutorial: Measuring audiovisual simultaneity"
summary: Compare Spearman–Kärber, probit and Psignifit methods for analysing audiovisual simultaneity judgements with MATLAB scripts and example data.
---

The TIMELY tutorial shows how to analyse judgements of audiovisual simultaneity when the timing difference between a flash and a sound varies. The examples compare the Spearman–Kärber method, probit analysis and Psignifit fitting without priors.

[Download the MATLAB scripts and example data (ZIP)](/pages/Timely_files/timely.zip).

## Responses at each audiovisual asynchrony

![Individual response proportions at each audiovisual asynchrony.](/pages/Timely_files/shapeimage_2.png)

![Pooled response proportions for each audiovisual asynchrony.](/pages/Timely_files/untitled.jpg)

Negative values indicate that sound came first. Positive values indicate that light came first. The [original tutorial figures](/pages/timely-archive.html) identify the subset of 15 participants and overlay their pooled results.
